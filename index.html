Admissions insights

Study in Italy: essential checklists & requirements

Document checklist for admission in Italy

  • Degrees & Transcripts
  • Passport
  • Recommendation Letters
  • Europass CV
  • English Proficiency (IELTS or University Certificate)
  • Motivation Letter
  • (Optional) Job Certificates

Financial requirement

The minimum financial requirement has been increased. €10,179.85/year must be shown as proof of funds.

  • Clearly reflected in bank statement
  • Funds legal and traceable
  • Preferably in student’s own account or family sponsor

Important: Financial documents and profiles will be strictly evaluated. Make sure your preparation is complete.

Educational documents attestation process

  1. Educational documents: Matric & Intermediate certificates, Bachelor’s degree & transcript.
  2. Board / university attestation first.
  3. IBCC / HEC attestation in person through appointments.
  4. MOFA attestation for educational documents.
  5. Apostille via MOFA, either by courier or in person.

Educational documents translation for Italy student visa

Once admission is confirmed and documents are apostilled, translate them early to avoid visa delays.
