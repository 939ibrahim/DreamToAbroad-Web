Cyber vergent

Cybersecurity compliance platform
About

Cybervergent is a cybersecurity compliance platform. It offers a comprehensive 360 degree security solutions for businesses and individuals.

Cybervergent platform simplifies and automates cybersecurity compliance, risk and governance for businesses, increasing the level of confidence individuals and businesses have in the security, privacy, and reliability of digital transactions.
Our task was to develop branding and a promo website for the template platform. The promo website consists of a home page, Pricing, and Contact Us.

Challenge
Cybersecurity is complex as hell, and everyone needs a unified solution for it. We had to explain tech protocols and frameworks to small businesses in a way they will trust.
The product is pretty big. We needed to explain all of it's features and advantages, and be specific. Non-tech B2B lead would not read 50 pages of tech data to get to the point, so the UX could not just follow the best practices praying for the custom to get to a CTA.
3D and animations. The client wanted to use complex 3Ds & parallax effect across the site, while maintaining high loading speed of the final result.

UX/UI

The entire cybersecurity ecosystem in one dashboard as the core value proposition, which we delivered through a straightforward and clear UI.

3D Modelling & Animation

as controversial as it sounds - the UX of the site had to revolve around animations & 3Ds. We've designed an interactive story for a user to take across the entire home page.

Page by page, section by section

Beginning with the sitemap, and through every section we meticulously explained product's functional & business sides, from simpler language and illustrations on the home page, to raw (though impressive) data on the specifically marked sections.

Branding Incorporated

The client came to us with a prepared branding kit which we had to seamlessly integrate into UI kit. We followed a set of the best practices, combining our professional input on UI direction, but ensuring it stays on brand.

Development

We used Webflow to do a pixel perfect implementation of the Figma-made custom design - developing the layout and embodying functionality through integrations.

We combined Webflow's server side & CMS with custom Javascript & ThreeJS

Establishing pixel perfect implementation of a fairly complex 3D-enriched UI.

Long story short - we succeeded!

Project timeline

Thanks to an integrated approach to projects and well-coordinated team processes, the project was completed within 3 months
